# ADR-0009: the lab's own PKI - etcd over TLS with authentication, PostgreSQL verified by a CA

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24

## Context

ADR-0005 left three things plain: etcd spoke http without authentication, PostgreSQL served a
self-signed certificate per node so clients got encryption without identity (`sslmode=require`), and
PgBouncer accepted clients in plain text. Read on the live lab: etcd 3.7.4 takes a certificate, a
key and a trusted CA for clients and for peers and has password authentication (`etcdctl auth`);
Patroni 4.1.5's `etcd3` section reads `protocol`, `cacert`, `username` and `password`; a certificate
signed by a CA with the node's address among its SANs passes `sslmode=verify-full` by address and by
name, and another node's self-signed one fails against it; PgBouncer 1.26.1 has `client_tls_*` and
`server_tls_*`; `/tmp` of the containers is tmpfs.

## Decision

`scripts/lab.sh pki` makes one CA for the lab in `.pki/` (gitignored, like `.ssh/`) and a certificate
per host with its name, its address, localhost and the VIP as SANs, remade when it no longer verifies
or lost its address. The roles place them: etcd serves TLS to clients and requires peer certificates
from the CA; authentication is enabled once with a root user and a `patroni` user limited to the
Patroni namespace (the passwords are two more generated secrets); Patroni reaches etcd over https
with the CA as that user. PostgreSQL serves the node's certificate with `ssl_ca_file`; the replicas
stream with `sslmode=verify-full`; PgBouncer verifies the primary with `verify-full` and speaks TLS
only to its clients with the balancer's certificate - a client at the VIP verifies it with
`verify-full`, which is how the drills and the probes connect. On real hosts the CA is the
organisation's and `.pki/` is what it issues.

## Consequences

Every connection of the data plane is encrypted and authenticated in both directions of trust:
the client knows the server, the server knows the password. A lost `.pki/` is a reset: etcd's
authentication and the certificates are bound to it. Client certificates (mTLS to PostgreSQL) are
not issued: passwords over verified TLS are the contract here. The REST API of Patroni, the
monitoring endpoints and the backup repository stay as ADR-0005 left them - the next step.
